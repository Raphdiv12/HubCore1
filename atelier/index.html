HUBCORE ATELIER

The first drawing is rarely the one we build.

Atelier is about the part in between: what we keep, what we redraw, and how we tell the difference.

A direct path meeting resistance, and the compass arc that found another way around.

01 / Attempt

A first version answers the brief. It is drawn before anything has pushed back.

02 / Reconsider

When something doesn't fit, the instinct is to push the first version harder. We've learned to stop and look at what is actually resisting.

03 / Redraw

The obstacle is not an obstruction to fight—it is the pivot. The compass finds its center on the resistance point.

04 / Realise

Often the thing in the way ends up deciding the shape. The first pass remains as pentimento beneath.

Staying with it

Most work goes through a stretch where it is unfinished and unconvincing, and progress is hard to see. We stay with it. That doesn't mean defending every early decision. It means keeping hold of what the work is for and letting the rest change.

Not every obstacle is useful. Some are just in the way. Telling them apart is most of the job.
